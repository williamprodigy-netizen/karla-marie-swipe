Worth taking

Pay for the first action

$500 to publish one ebook. For us the equivalent is paying a new creator for landing their first brand deal or shipping their first portfolio piece. It converts a refund-risk window into a proof-generation window, and it makes the guarantee conversation moot because the member has already been paid.

Sweeten live rather than discount

She doubles the credits mid-pitch instead of cutting price. The perceived value moves without touching the number.

Visual before numeric

The investment donut lands the emotional comparison before the price is spoken. Cheap to reproduce in any deck.

Objection pre-empted by name

“This definitely is not a timeshare thing.” She names the exact suspicion the bonus creates and kills it in one line.

Not worth taking

The fake-live framing, and the resort bonus itself — it is a third-party lead-gen arrangement dressed as generosity, and it invites precisely the scepticism she has to spend a minute of pitch time defusing.
